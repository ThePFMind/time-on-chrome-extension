Chrome extension · for test evidence

Time On

A small glass clock in the corner of every page, with the date and the system you are testing. Every screenshot you take for a test result carries its own timestamp and stage. Try it below: change the look on the right, drag the grip to move it, double-click the grip to reset.

Look (Options > Look)

The tag follows the URL, using the rules in the table below.

Four stages, four colours

With no rules set, the tag is the site name and the colour comes from a word in it. The word must stand alone between dots or dashes: app-dev matches, appdev does not.

SBXSandboxsbx · sandbox · trial · playground
DEVDevelopmentdev · develop · localhost · local
TESTTesttest · qa · qas · uat · stage · staging · stg · preprod
PRODProductionprod · prd · production · live

Name your own systems

Options > System tag rules. Each rule is a regex on the full URL; the first match wins. In the tag text, $1 is the first regex group, {host} the host name, and {client} the sap-client from the URL, else the rule's default client. The colour is fixed per rule, or Auto from the host words. Try the URLs in the demo:

Install

  1. From the Chrome Web Store: open the Time On listing and click Add to Chrome. Your team gets the link once the listing is live.
  2. Before that, from the folder: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the clock-overlay folder.
  3. Pin the toolbar icon. Click it, or press Alt+Shift+K, to show or hide the clock. A badge reading OFF means it is hidden.

Share rules with the team

  1. One person writes the rules in Options and clicks Export rules. That saves time-on-rules.json.
  2. Everyone else opens Options, clicks Import rules, chooses the file and clicks Save.
  3. Rules, the Look and the show-on list sync with your Chrome profile. On/off and the clock position are kept per PC and per site.
